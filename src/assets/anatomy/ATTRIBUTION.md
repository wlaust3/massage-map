The figure is a deliberately simplified, stylized front and back muscular illustration. Each muscle is its own
path with a stable id (see `src/data/muscles.ts`), and the right-hand side of every figure is the mirror image of the
left-hand side, generated in code.

It is functional rather than medically precise. An artist-drawn or open-licensed (CC0 / CC-BY) upgrade would
improve it. If art is swapped in, keep the muscle ids stable and record the new source, author, license and any
modifications in this file.
